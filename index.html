Naicheng Yu

I am a master's student in Computer Science and Engineering at UC San Diego. Before UCSD, I received my B.E. in Software Engineering from Zhejiang University.

My research focuses on LLM agents and benchmarking and evaluation. I am particularly interested in building better agent systems and designing reliable benchmarks and evaluation methods for measuring and understanding their capabilities.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 and am always happy to discuss research ideas or potential collaborations.
